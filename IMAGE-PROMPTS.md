# Image generation brief

Created with the built-in image generation tool for the MarqByte website. Seven original landscape illustrations; project files are assets/dich-vu-{ads,zalo,seo,marketing,website,tools,social}.webp with 600px variants. Original image generation prompt shared style: polished editorial 3D still life, matte ceramic and frosted glass; emerald green, lime, ivory and charcoal; soft studio daylight; no logos, typography or watermark; landscape 3:2. These are service concept illustrations, not evidence of real projects.

## ads

Digital advertising campaign desk sculpture: large green megaphone, target with arrow, floating ad creative frames, cursor and search advertising cards. Communicate multi-platform paid advertising.

## zalo

Customer communication still life: white smartphone angled on a green plinth, layered green message bubbles, contact profile tiles, subtle handshake motif and notification bell. Customer relationship and official business messaging.

## seo

Website SEO still life: open ivory laptop showing abstract green search result rows, a large magnifying glass focusing a webpage, small branching site structure sculpture and leaves. Search visibility and website care.

## marketing

Integrated marketing strategy still life: large round green strategy table with a compass, modular campaign planning cards, small megaphone, a calendar board, and connected green pathways. Coordination and strategic planning.

## website

Web design still life: floating elegant desktop screen, tablet and smartphone displaying a consistent white-and-green landing page with abstract blocks; tiny typography ruler, cursor and component tiles. Responsive website design.

## tools

Business automation still life: interlocking green gears, ivory laptop with modular workflow blocks, a silver robotic gripper arranging data cubes, connected database cylinders. Practical bespoke business software tools.

## social

Social media creative still life: floating square visual post frames, green camera, clapperboard, speech bubble, heart sculpture, content calendar tiles and expressive emerald ribbons. Brand content production.
